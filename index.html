01 / A little about me

It started with
a YouTube channel
in 2009.

I started making YouTube videos in 2009, teaching myself to edit because I wanted to connect with people. That passion grew into collaborative radio episodes and video projects that brought more than 300 people together. I love turning an idea into something people want to be part of.

My love of beauty began with my own skin. Severe acne in high school led me to explore skincare, and I know how much skin concerns can affect someone’s confidence. I could happily talk about beauty all day—but what matters most to me is helping people discover products that could make their everyday lives better.

My experience as a Global Marketer at APR brought my passions for beauty and content together. I want to help beauty brands tell stories that people see themselves in—making products feel relevant, approachable, and worth discovering.

01 / OWNERSHIP

Think like it’s
my brand.

I cared about the brand well beyond my desk. Even with a daily commute that could take up to six hours, I kept following Instagram trends and the beauty industry. Each morning began with checking product sales. Each week, I analysed viral TikToks and quickly turned relevant ideas into plans for new ad creatives.

Inspiration could come from anywhere. An ice-cream ad on an elevator screen became the starting point for a creative I made for our own products. On weekend visits to Olive Young, I photographed the ads outside and thought about how to make our products just as compelling.

I saw the brand’s possibilities everywhere because I cared about it as if it were my own.

03 / GROWTH

Stay curious.

Through collaborations with different brands, I’ve learned to adapt my creative approach to each brand’s identity. I study their social media and follow their guidelines to understand the tone, visual style, and feeling they want to convey. I bring those observations into filming and editing, creating content that feels true to the brand.
